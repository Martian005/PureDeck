# PureDeck v1.0

First public release.

## What's in it

- Hero backdrop that changes as you move over streaming apps: recently added and trending titles for your country, drawn with each show's own logo
- Glass dock and app grid, with an actions sheet for each app
- Search with details, ratings, where to stream, and "Open" straight into Netflix, Prime Video and others
- Built-in settings card (clock, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, screensaver, sorting)
- Ad-free, no accounts, no analytics

## Install

Download `PureDeck.apk` below, or in Downloader enter
`https://github.com/Martian005/PureDeck/releases/latest/download/PureDeck.apk`.
Requires Android 8.0+. Steps for making it the default home screen, and for undoing that, are in the README.

## Notes

- The APK uses the author's catalogue server. It may be slow or go offline; deploy your own for anything permanent ([guide](https://github.com/Martian005/PureDeck/blob/main/docs/DEPLOY-BACKEND.md)).
- Tested mostly on an Android TV 8 set. Google TV has had less testing.
- Use at your own risk. See the README.
